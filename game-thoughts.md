# Thoughts on the game

I'd like this to have CCG elements like in magic the gathering.

it should be a deck construction game, with a deck builder and a deck editor.

it should also be sort of like starcraft, where you can build your own army and battle other players.

the narrative could be that you're a space trader and you get a new "card" every turn - you start with 7 cards - there should be resource gathering units, combat units, and other units.

you collect resources and use them to cast the cards - you can only cast cards that you have in your hand.

it should have hex grid mechanics, where you can move your units around the grid and interact with the environment.

it should be turn based. you collect resources and use them to cast the cards. the cards should have various costs and effects.

the units should interact with each other in ways that are sort of like synergistic cards and combo situations. eg, like when you have academy manufactor and nuka cola vending machine out in MTG and both are on the field you get good synergy, and add an ashnod's alter and it goes infinite. there should be fun animations for these synergisted actions.
